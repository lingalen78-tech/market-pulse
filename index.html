2026-04-06 總經觀察

聯準會暫停升息後的市場展望：多空交錯的第二季

隨著聯準會在三月會議後決定維持利率不變，市場進入了一個微妙的觀望期。S&P 500 在突破前高後出現了量能不足的跡象，技術面上 RSI 已經進入超買區域，短期回調的可能性正在增加。

值得注意的是，債券殖利率曲線的變化暗示著市場對經濟前景的看法出現分歧。10 年期與 2 年期利差持續收窄，這通常是經濟放緩的先行指標。建議投資人在第二季保持謹慎，適度降低槓桿比例。

操作建議：短線偏空看待，支撐位關注 5,200 點。若跌破則可能下探 5,050 區域。多頭若要重新進場，需等待放量突破 5,400。

2026-04-06 Macro Outlook

Market Outlook After the Fed Pause: A Mixed Q2

With the Federal Reserve deciding to hold rates steady after its March meeting, the market has entered a delicate wait-and-see period. The S&P 500 showed signs of weakening volume after breaking previous highs, and technically the RSI has entered overbought territory, increasing the probability of a short-term pullback.

Notably, changes in the bond yield curve suggest divergent views on the economic outlook. The 10-year to 2-year spread continues to narrow, which is typically a leading indicator of economic slowdown. We recommend investors remain cautious in Q2 and moderately reduce leverage.

Trading Suggestion: Short-term bearish bias. Watch support at 5,200. A break below could test the 5,050 area. Bulls should wait for a high-volume breakout above 5,400 before re-entering.

2026-04-03 科技股

AI 晶片需求持續增長，NVDA 與 TSM 技術面解讀

NVIDIA 在最新財報後股價創下歷史新高，數據中心收入的爆發性增長超出市場預期。從技術面來看，NVDA 週線呈現強勢的多頭排列，5 週均線與 20 週均線持續擴張，MACD 柱狀體在零軸上方放大。

台積電 ADR（TSM）受惠於 AI 訂單的強勁增長，股價在月線級別已確認突破長期箱型整理。然而短期 RSI 已達 72，接近超買區間，追高風險偏高。建議關注回測前高作為支撐時的進場機會。

關注焦點：本週 MSFT 與 GOOGL 將公布財報，若雲端業務數據不如預期，可能引發科技股整體修正。

2026-04-03 Tech Stocks

AI Chip Demand Continues to Surge: NVDA & TSM Technical Analysis

NVIDIA hit all-time highs following its latest earnings report, with explosive data center revenue growth exceeding market expectations. Technically, NVDA's weekly chart shows a strong bullish alignment — the 5-week and 20-week moving averages continue to diverge, with the MACD histogram expanding above the zero line.

TSMC's ADR (TSM) benefited from robust AI order growth, with the monthly chart confirming a breakout from a long-term consolidation range. However, the short-term RSI has reached 72, approaching overbought territory, making chasing highs risky. Watch for a retest of the previous high as support for entry opportunities.

Key Watch: MSFT and GOOGL report earnings this week. If cloud business numbers disappoint, it could trigger a broader tech correction.

2026-03-30 原物料

黃金突破 2,400 後的策略思考

黃金價格在地緣政治風險與降息預期的雙重推動下，成功站上 2,400 美元關口。從量化角度來看，月線級別的布林帶正在擴張，暗示趨勢可能加速。然而日線 RSI 已連續三天處於超買區域，短期拉回風險不可忽視。

原油方面，WTI 受到 OPEC+ 減產協議延長的支撐，但全球經濟放緩的隱憂讓多頭動能受限。目前油價在 80-85 美元區間震盪，突破方向將取決於本月公布的需求數據。

操作建議：黃金維持多頭思維，但短線建議等待回測 2,350 支撐再加碼。原油觀望為主，等待方向確認。

2026-03-30 Commodities

Strategic Thinking After Gold Breaks $2,400

Gold prices successfully breached the $2,400 level, driven by a combination of geopolitical risks and rate-cut expectations. From a quantitative perspective, the monthly Bollinger Bands are expanding, suggesting the trend could accelerate. However, the daily RSI has been in overbought territory for three consecutive days — short-term pullback risk should not be ignored.

On the crude oil front, WTI is supported by the extension of the OPEC+ production cut agreement, but concerns about global economic slowdown are limiting bullish momentum. Oil is currently oscillating in the $80–85 range, with the breakout direction depending on demand data to be released this month.

Trading Suggestion: Maintain a bullish bias on gold, but wait for a retest of the $2,350 support level before adding positions. Stay on the sidelines for crude oil until direction is confirmed.

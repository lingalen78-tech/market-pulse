2026 Q2 US Equity Outlook: AI Bubble or the New Normal?

2026 Q2 美股展望：AI 泡沫還是新常態？

The Magnificent 7 now account for over 35% of the S&P 500's market cap. That concentration hasn't been seen since the dot-com era. But here's why I think this time is different — and where the risks actually are.

The P/E ratio (price-to-earnings — how much investors pay per dollar of profit) for the NASDAQ sits at 32x, compared to a 10-year average of 25x. Expensive? Yes. But unlike 2000, these companies are generating real cash flows. NVIDIA alone is expected to earn $90B+ in revenue this year from actual AI infrastructure demand.

The real risk isn't that AI is fake — it's that the capex cycle (capital expenditure — how much companies invest in infrastructure) could slow if ROI doesn't materialize for enterprise customers. Watch Microsoft and Google cloud earnings this quarter for signals.

My take: Selectively bullish on AI infrastructure (semis, cloud), cautious on AI application plays with no earnings. The trend is real, but not every stock riding the wave deserves a 40x multiple.

「Magnificent 7」（七大科技巨頭）現在佔 S&P 500 總市值超過 35%。這種集中度自網路泡沫以來未曾見過。但我認為這次不同——風險其實在別的地方。

那斯達克的 本益比（P/E ratio）（投資人願意為每一塊錢獲利付出多少錢）目前是 32 倍，而過去十年平均是 25 倍。貴嗎？是。但不像 2000 年，這些公司有真正的現金流。光 NVIDIA 今年預估營收就超過 $900 億美元，來自實際的 AI 基礎建設需求。

真正的風險不在 AI 是假的——而是資本支出週期（capex cycle）（公司在基礎建設上投入多少錢）可能放緩，如果企業客戶的投資報酬率沒跟上的話。這季留意微軟和 Google 雲端財報的訊號。

我的看法：選擇性看多 AI 基礎建設（半導體、雲端），對沒有獲利的 AI 應用股保持謹慎。趨勢是真的，但不是每檔搭上 AI 浪潮的股票都值得 40 倍本益比。

Gold Breaks $3,000: Safe Haven or Inflation Signal?

黃金突破 $3,000：避險需求還是通膨信號？

Gold just crossed the psychologically important $3,000 mark. The financial media is split: is this about fear, or about inflation? My answer: it's about something bigger — a structural shift in how central banks think about reserves.

Since 2022, central banks — led by China, India, and Turkey — have been buying gold at a pace not seen in 50 years. This is de-dollarization (countries reducing their dependence on the US dollar) in action. They're not reacting to CPI prints; they're repositioning for a multipolar currency world.

For retail investors, the real yield (bond yield minus inflation — what you actually earn after accounting for rising prices) is the key variable. When real yields fall, gold becomes more attractive because holding cash or bonds earns you less. Right now, real yields are declining as the Fed signals rate cuts.

My take: Gold is in a secular bull market. Short-term pullbacks are buying opportunities. Target: $3,200 by year-end. The biggest risk? A surprise hawkish pivot from the Fed — unlikely given current data.

黃金剛突破心理關卡 $3,000。財經媒體意見分歧：這是恐慌驅動，還是通膨信號？我的答案：是更大的東西——央行對儲備思維的結構性轉變。

從 2022 年起，以中國、印度和土耳其為首的各國央行，正以 50 年來最快速度買進黃金。這就是去美元化（de-dollarization）（各國減少對美元的依賴）的實際行動。他們不是在反應 CPI 數字；而是在為多極貨幣世界做準備。

對散戶投資人來說，實質殖利率（real yield）（債券殖利率減掉通膨——考慮物價上漲後你實際賺到多少）是關鍵變數。實質殖利率下降時，黃金更有吸引力，因為持有現金或債券賺得更少。目前聯準會釋出降息訊號，實質殖利率正在走低。

我的看法：黃金處於長期多頭市場。短期拉回是買進機會。目標：年底前 $3,200。最大風險？聯準會意外轉鷹——以目前數據來看不太可能。

TAIEX Above 21,000: Semiconductor-Led or Broad Bull Market?

台股加權站上兩萬一：半導體帶動還是全面牛市？

The TAIEX has been on a tear, pushing past 21,000 for the first time. But peel back the index, and you'll notice something important: this rally has a very specific driver.

TSMC alone accounts for about 30% of the TAIEX's weight. When TSMC moves, the index moves. The company's forward P/E (price relative to next year's expected earnings — tells you how much you're paying for future growth) is at 22x, which is reasonable given its near-monopoly in advanced chips.

The concern: market breadth (how many stocks are participating in the rally — a healthy market has broad participation) tells a different story. Only about 40% of listed stocks are above their 200-day moving average. That means most of the market isn't keeping up with the headline index.

My take: This is a semiconductor-led rally, not a broad bull market. If you own TSMC and its supply chain, you're happy. If you're in traditional sectors, you might be wondering why your portfolio doesn't match the headlines. For new entries: wait for breadth to improve before going all-in on the index.

台股加權指數一路飆升，首次站上 21,000。但仔細看指數內部，你會發現一件重要的事：這波漲勢有非常明確的推手。

光是台積電就佔了加權指數約 30% 的權重。台積電動，指數就動。台積電的預估本益比（forward P/E）（股價相對於明年預期獲利——告訴你為未來成長付了多少錢）在 22 倍，考慮到它在先進製程的近乎壟斷地位，算是合理的。

值得擔憂的是：市場寬度（market breadth）（有多少股票參與漲勢——健康的市場是多數股票一起漲）說了不同的故事。只有約 40% 的上市股票站在 200 日均線以上，代表大部分股票跟不上指數的腳步。

我的看法：這是半導體帶動的漲勢，不是全面性牛市。如果你持有台積電和它的供應鏈，你很開心。如果你在傳統產業，你可能在想為什麼你的投資組合跟頭條數字對不上。新進場的人：等市場寬度改善再考慮大舉加碼指數。

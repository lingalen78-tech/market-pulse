研究筆記Research notes 以下是閱讀財報時可追問的問題，非當期結論或買賣評級，不隨每日行情更新。Questions for reading company reports, not current conclusions or trade ratings. These notes do not refresh with daily prices.

資訊科技Information Technology
XLK
研究雲端與晶片業務時，分開看營收成長、毛利率與資本支出。Separate revenue growth, gross margins and capital spending when reviewing cloud and chip businesses.
醫療保健Health Care
XLV
藥品、醫材與醫療服務的收入模式不同；比較時先確認業務組合。Drug makers, device companies and care providers have different revenue models. Check the business mix before comparing them.
金融Financials
XLF
銀行可從淨利差、存款成本與信用損失準備開始比較。For banks, start with net interest margins, deposit costs and credit-loss provisions.
非必需消費Consumer Discretionary
XLY
銷量、售價與促銷費用如何影響毛利？把需求變動和價格效果分開看。How do volumes, prices and promotions affect gross profit? Separate demand changes from pricing effects.
通訊服務Communication Services
XLC
廣告收入與訂閱收入分開看，再檢查內容支出與用戶留存。Review advertising and subscription revenue separately, then examine content spending and customer retention.
工業Industrials
XLI
訂單不等於收入。檢查訂單轉換、產能利用與營運資金。Orders are not revenue. Review backlog conversion, capacity use and working capital.
必需消費Consumer Staples
XLP
比較銷量與價格對營收的貢獻，以及原料成本能否轉嫁。Compare volume and pricing contributions to sales, and whether input costs can be passed through.
能源Energy
XLE
比較油氣價格假設、開採成本與維持產量所需的資本支出。Compare commodity-price assumptions, production costs and the capital spending needed to maintain output.
公用事業Utilities
XLU
從監管報酬、負債成本與電網投資需求檢查公用事業。Review regulated returns, borrowing costs and grid investment requirements.
房地產Real Estate
XLRE
租金、出租率、租約到期與再融資成本，應按物業類型拆開比較。Compare rents, occupancy, lease expirations and refinancing costs by property type.
原物料Materials
XLB
把商品售價、投入成本與出貨量分開，避免只看營收增減。Separate commodity prices, input costs and shipment volumes instead of relying on revenue growth alone.

技術指標定義Indicator Definitions

RSI(14)：先以 14 筆價格變動的平均漲幅、跌幅起算，後續採 Wilder 平滑：新平均 =（前平均 × 13 + 本次漲／跌幅）÷ 14。RSI = 100 − 100 ÷（1 + 平均漲幅 ÷ 平均跌幅）。
≥70 超買，≤30 超賣；不是買賣指令。全平盤設為 50，只有漲幅為 100，只有跌幅為 0。
SMA50/200：最近 50／200 筆收盤價的簡單平均。資料來源、復權與起算日不同，數值仍可能與其他平台不同。

RSI(14): seed with mean gains and losses over 14 price changes, then apply Wilder smoothing: new average = (previous average × 13 + current gain/loss) ÷ 14. RSI = 100 − 100 ÷ (1 + average gain ÷ average loss).
≥70 is overbought; ≤30 is oversold, not a trade instruction. Flat sequences return 50, only gains 100 and only losses 0.
SMA50/200: means of the latest 50/200 closes. Sources, adjustments and initialization dates can still cause differences across platforms.

情緒指數公式與分級Sentiment formulas and thresholds

clip(x) = min(100, max(0, x))

  • RSI: clip((SPY RSI14 − 30) × 2 + 10)
  • VIX: clip(90 − (VIX − 10) × 80/30)
  • SMA200: clip(50 + (SPY / SMA200 − 1) × 100 × 5)

三項各占 1/3，平均後取最接近整數（正好 .5 時取偶數）。缺少任何一項，停止整次行情更新並保留先前快照。分級：0–24 極度恐慌、25–44 恐慌、45–54 中性、55–74 貪婪、75–100 極度貪婪。權重與門檻是本站設定，沒有預測績效的回測。Average all three with equal 1/3 weights and round to the nearest integer (ties to even). Missing any input aborts the data update and retains the prior snapshot. Labels: 0–24 extreme fear, 25–44 fear, 45–54 neutral, 55–74 greed, 75–100 extreme greed. Weights and thresholds are site conventions, without a predictive backtest.
